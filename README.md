## About

I'm a second-year Design and Creation of Interactive Products student at La Salle Barcelona. I work where product design, interaction and frontend meet: working out how something should behave, designing the interface, and building a version people can actually use.

Most of my projects start from a real frustration, mine or someone close to me. I prototype, test on real devices, and change the design when the implementation shows a better answer. I use AI to explore and build faster, and I check everything it gives me before it ships.

**Open to internships from June 2027, or through a university agreement**, in product design, UX engineering and frontend. Barcelona, abroad or remote.

A training screen for a garage gym, controlled by pointing at the camera so chalked hands never touch it.

## How I work

- **Start from a real frustration**, compare a few ways to solve it, build the one that holds up, and write down what is still a guess.
- **Test on the real thing.** Real devices, real data, real people where I can get them. Simulators and mockups hide the problems that matter.
- **Take it apart before touching it.** When something breaks I isolate each piece first. It is faster than guessing.
